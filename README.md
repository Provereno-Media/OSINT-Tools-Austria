# OSINT-Tools-Austria
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/41/Flag_of_Austria.svg" alt="Austria_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Austria. Most of the resources and tools has versions in German; many also in English. Basic company information (Kurzinformation) from the Firmenbuch is free on JustizOnline, while full extracts and land register (Grundbuch) extracts are paid. The register of beneficial owners (WiEReG) is open to the press and to civil-society organisations working against money laundering, which are presumed to have a legitimate interest. Enter names with German umlauts (ä, ö, ü, ß).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [data.gv.at](https://www.data.gv.at/) — national open data portal with datasets from federal, state and city administrations. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Statistik Austria — Open Data](https://data.statistik.gv.at/web/) — Statistics Austria's official statistics in machine-readable formats: demography, economy, prices and regional data. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [OeNB — Datenangebot (Austrian National Bank — Data)](https://www.oenb.at/daten-und-forschung/datenangebot.html) — central bank statistics on interest rates, banking, financial markets and the external sector. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [RIS — Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes (Federal Legal Information System)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/) — federal and state law in force, official gazettes and decisions of the Supreme Court, Constitutional Court, Administrative Court and other courts and authorities ([case law](https://ris.bka.gv.at/Judikatur/)). *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Ediktsdatei (Edicts Database)](https://edikte.justiz.gv.at/) — Ministry of Justice's official publication of insolvency proceedings, court auctions of real estate and other judicial notices. *Lang: DE.*
- [evi.gv.at (Official Gazette)](https://www.evi.gv.at/) — Electronic Publication and Information Platform of the federal government, the official gazette of the Republic of Austria, with notification subscriptions. *Lang: DE.*
- [Wiener Börse (Vienna Stock Exchange)](https://www.wienerborse.at/) — listed companies, prices, issuer news and indices. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Rechnungshof (Court of Audit)](https://www.rechnungshof.gv.at/) — audit reports on federal, state and municipal bodies and state-owned companies; also publishes party financial statements. *Lang: DE, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [JustizOnline — Firmenbuchabfrage (Companies Register)](https://justizonline.gv.at/jop/web/firmenbuchabfrage) — Ministry of Justice access to the Firmenbuch: free short information (name, legal form, seat, address, FN number, representatives, insolvency note); full and historical extracts are paid. *Lang: DE.*
- [WKO Firmen A-Z (Chamber of Commerce Directory)](https://firmen.wko.at/SearchSimple.aspx) — directory of about 629,000 Austrian businesses with contacts, trades and locations. *Lang: DE.*
- [GISA — Gewerbeinformationssystem Austria (Trade Register)](https://www.gisa.gv.at/) — nationwide register of trade licences (since 2015), including sole traders not in the Firmenbuch. *Lang: DE.*
- [FMA — Company Database (Financial Market Authority)](https://www.fma.gv.at/en/search-company-database/) — banks, insurers, investment firms and other supervised or registered entities. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Firmenatlas](https://www.firmenatlas.com/) — free access to Firmenbuch data: companies and linked persons, functions, shareholdings and annual accounts; changes and insolvencies. *Lang: DE.*
- [Lobbying- und Interessenvertretungs-Register (Lobbying Register)](https://lobbyreg.justiz.gv.at/) — Ministry of Justice register of lobbying companies, lobbyists and interest groups. *Lang: DE.*
- [Compass](https://compass.at/de) — commercial business information provider since 1867: company, trade, land register and compliance data; mostly paid. *Lang: DE.*
- [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/) — North Data covers company data in Austria as part of its XL package, which includes comprehensive information on companies, key persons, and financials. *Lang: EN, DE, FR.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [JustizOnline — Grundbuchabfrage (Land Register)](https://justizonline.gv.at/jop/web/grundbuchabfrage) — Ministry of Justice access to the land register: owners, encumbrances and mortgages by cadastral municipality and plot or address. Paid extract. *Lang: DE.*
- [Austrian Map (BEV)](https://maps.bev.gv.at/) — Federal Office of Metrology and Surveying's map viewer with topographic maps and the cadastral map (plot boundaries and numbers). *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [basemap.at](https://basemap.at/) — official administrative base map of Austria from the federal states, with orthophotos. *Lang: DE.*
- [Stadtplan Wien (Vienna City Map)](https://www.wien.gv.at/stadtplan/) — Vienna's GIS: zoning plans (Flächenwidmung), cultural heritage, environment and transport layers. *Lang: DE.*
- [DORIS interMAP (Upper Austria)](https://www.doris.at/) — Upper Austria's geographic information system, an example of the state geoportals. *Lang: DE.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- No public vehicle-owner search exists in Austria; owner data are available only to authorities and parties with a legal claim (e.g. after an accident).

### Maritime
- [DoRIS — Donau River Information Services (viadonau)](https://www.doris.bmimi.gv.at/) — information platform for Danube navigation: water levels, lock status, fairway availability and inland AIS-based services. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Austro Control — Luftfahrzeugregister Online-Abfrage (Aircraft Register Query)](https://www.austrocontrol.at/luftfahrtbehoerde/luftfahrzeuge/lfz_register/online-abfrage) — online query of the Austrian civil aircraft register (OE- registrations); by law the register records manufacturer, type, serial number, MTOW and the holder's name and address. *Lang: DE.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Herold Telefonbuch](https://www.herold.at/telefonbuch/austria/) — telephone directory of persons and businesses with addresses and phone numbers. *Lang: DE.*
- [Parlament Österreich — Personen (Parliament — Persons)](https://www.parlament.gv.at/recherchieren/personen) — members of the National Council and Federal Council, past and present, with biographies and functions. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [BMI — Wahlen (Ministry of the Interior — Elections)](https://www.bmi.gv.at/412/) — official results of National Council, presidential, European and referendum votes. *Lang: DE.*
- [Gesundheitsberuferegister (Register of Health Professions)](https://gbr-public.ehealth.gv.at/) — public register of nurses, therapists and other health professions; search by name, registration number or profession. *Lang: DE.*
- [ÖRAK — Rechtsanwalt finden (Austrian Bar — Find a Lawyer)](https://www.oerak.at/buergerservice/servicecorner/rechtsanwalt-finden/) — search of lawyers registered with the Austrian bar associations. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Meldeauskunft (Central Residents Register Inquiry)](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/personen__meldeauskunft) — anyone can request the main residence of a clearly identified person from the Central Register of Residents, for a fee. *Lang: DE.*
- [Matricula Online](https://data.matricula-online.eu/de/) — digitised church registers (baptisms, marriages, deaths) from Austria and neighbouring countries. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [ANNO — AustriaN Newspapers Online](https://anno.onb.ac.at/) — Austrian National Library's full-text archive of historical newspapers and magazines. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [USP — Ausschreibungssuche (Business Service Portal Tender Search)](https://www.usp.gv.at/en/services/ausschreibungssuche.html) — government search of tenders published under the Federal Procurement Act 2018. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [Bundesbeschaffung GmbH (Federal Procurement Agency)](https://www.bbg.gv.at/) — central purchasing body: framework agreements and the e-shop for public buyers. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [RTR — Medientransparenz (Media Transparency Database)](https://www.rtr.at/medien/was_wir_tun/medientransparenz/startseite.de.html) — quarterly disclosures under the Media Transparency Act: advertising orders and media subsidies from bodies audited by the Court of Audit, by payer and media outlet. *Lang: DE.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU procurement-notice search; filter by country Austria for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [nic.at — Whois-Abfrage](https://www.nic.at/en/my-at-domain/domain-search/whois) — official .at registry WHOIS: domain holder (where public), technical contact, name servers and DNSSEC data. *Lang: DE, EN.*
- [IANA — .at Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/at.html) — official delegation data for .at: registry operator, contacts and WHOIS server `whois.nic.at`. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Austrian networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
